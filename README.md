# Discover (Frum Feed)

> **A clean, unified news reader for Jewish headlines, announcements, and Zmanim.**

**Discover** (working title: *Frum Feed*) brings together real-time updates and community announcements from top Jewish media outlets into a single, sleek experience. Designed with filtered connectivity and distraction-free reading in mind, the app ensures you stay informed safely and reliably.

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Multi-Source Aggregation:** Pulls the latest headlines and announcements directly from:
  * *Yeshiva World News (YWN)*
  * *Anash.org*
  * *COLlive*
  * *CH Info*
* **Integrated Zmanim:** Check daily halachic times localized to your location alongside your daily feed.
* **100% Kosher & Self-Contained:** Zero external link redirects. Articles are rendered natively within the app, keeping browsing safe and compatible with all web filters.
* **Powerful Search Engine:** Search through past articles easily by keywords, dates, or specific outlets.
* **Offline Access:** Full offline support caches articles automatically so you can keep reading on the go without an internet connection.
